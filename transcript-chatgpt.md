### 🥇 Why I'd pick the ChatGPT version

The biggest advantage is that it reads like someone who actually does Market Intelligence, rather than someone trying to reposition a digital-marketing background into Market Intelligence.

The opening is particularly strong: *"Market Intelligence Analyst specialising in competitive intelligence, industry trends, market sizing, and data-driven research."* It immediately establishes the target role, while the next lines substantiate it with the 4,949-posting / 10-country analysis, Python, NLP, Power BI and stakeholder reporting.

More importantly, the experience section connects the current job to MI work: competitive positioning, industry trend monitoring, market shifts, performance intelligence, executive-ready recommendations, cross-functional analytical reporting. That is exactly the bridge a recruiter needs to see when moving from Strategic Accounts / Digital Marketing → Market Intelligence Analyst.

And the project section is excellent for this role. The AI Hiring project demonstrates: data collection → market analysis → segmentation → market sizing → dashboard → deployed intelligence tool. That is unusually strong evidence for an early-career MI candidate.

### 🥈 Claude Resume

Claude's version is actually very good, especially if the employer strongly prefers a one-page resume. Its strongest feature is the opening: *"Built a market intelligence pipeline that scraped, classified, and segmented 4,949 LinkedIn job postings across 10 countries…"* That's a fantastic hook because it leads with evidence rather than a generic career objective.

However, I prefer ChatGPT's version because it gives the recruiter more context around the actual professional experience. Claude compresses SocialBeat into only two bullets and leaves out the cross-functional reporting responsibility. So I'd use Claude's sharpness, but ChatGPT's content architecture.

### 🥉 Gemini Resume

Gemini's version has some good language, particularly *"transform raw web data into executive insights,"* and the skills are nicely aligned to MI. But I wouldn't submit this one as-is.

The biggest issue is the statement: *"Built an end-to-end market tracking engine scraping 8,800+ job postings…"* The other versions give the much more defensible 4,949 postings + 3,897 additional postings, whereas "8,800+" obscures the actual methodology.

There are also a few phrases that feel slightly more embellished than the evidence in the other resumes — for example, "consumer sentiment" and "published co-authored research poster." The underlying work is impressive enough that stretching the wording isn't necessary.

### The interesting part: I'd actually combine them

If this were the final resume for an actual Market Intelligence Analyst application, I'd take: **ChatGPT Resume's structure + Claude's punchiness + Gemini's strongest wording.**

**Keep from ChatGPT:** the Market Intelligence Analyst positioning, SocialBeat bullets, 4,949 + 3,897 methodology, TAM/SAM/SOM, Power BI dashboard, JD scoring tool, full skills section.

**Borrow from Claude:** the very first sentence — *"Built a market intelligence pipeline that scraped, classified, and segmented 4,949 LinkedIn job postings across 10 countries…"* — a stronger opening than simply stating the title.

**Avoid from Gemini:** "8,800+" instead of the precise numbers; claims that aren't directly demonstrated; overly buzzword-heavy phrasing.
